Same flag, but each switch is now genuinely bistable (café-style self-activation + a morphogen bias) instead of a smooth readout. Two things appear that the graded version can't show: boundaries snap sharp, and the switches show hysteresis — each trips ON at a higher morphogen level than it trips OFF. So the fate of a cell depends on its history. A naive field (switches start off, swept up ▸) and a primed field (switches start on, swept down ◂) draw different flags from the very same gradient. The shaded zones are where they disagree — that gap is the memory.
