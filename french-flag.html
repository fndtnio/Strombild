One morphogen gradient, read against two thresholds, yields three territories. The model is single-compartment, so position along the gradient is the value of morphogen — swept left to right below. At each level the two switches settle, and their on/off combination is the fate. The band boundaries are placed by the engine's own knife-edge math, not drawn by hand — and note the fourth combination (B on, A off) never appears, the tell that this is one ordered input, not two independent switches.
